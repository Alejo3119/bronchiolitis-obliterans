What is bronchiolitis obliterans?

Bronchiolitis obliterans is a disease that affects the bronchioles, the SMALLEST airways in the lungs. These airways become inflamed and, over time, can scar. That scarring makes them narrower and narrower, so air passes with more and more difficulty when leaving the lungs.Cleveland Clinic. (2026). Popcorn lung (bronchiolitis obliterans). Cleveland Clinic. https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22590-popcorn-lung-bronchiolitis-obliterans

When it happens after a bone marrow transplant, it is known as BRONCHIOLITIS OBLITERANS SYNDROME. It is one of the most serious manifestations of chronic graft-versus-host disease, though not the only one — for example, all my nails also fell out, my mouth filled with sores, I got patches all over my body, and my liver became inflamed. This happens because my body rejected the cells, which in this case were my brother's, even though we were 100% compatible. But the worst damage was to my lungs.Dinh-Van L. (2026). Why does it happen?

Bronchiolitis obliterans can have different causes. Some people develop it from exposure to irritating chemicals at work, like ammonia or chlorine. It can also appear after serious respiratory infections, transplants, or certain autoimmune diseases.National Jewish Health. (2025). Bronchiolitis obliterans. National Jewish Health. https://www.nationaljewish.org/conditions/bronchiolitis

In my case, I was first diagnosed with Hyper-IgM Syndrome, a genetic immunodeficiency that makes it hard to produce antibodies normally — antibodies being an essential part of your defenses. Basically, my defenses were dumb: for example, I'd catch a cold, and they'd beat it, but then the same cold could come back and they wouldn't know how to fight it, which isn't normal, because after a given cold X they're supposed to remember it, like a memory. The transplant could be a possible cure; the other option was to keep getting antibody infusions every month and leave it up to fate whether I'd stay alive with just that, which I don't think I would have.Fernandez J. (2026). Hyper-IgM syndrome. MSD Manual Consumer Version. https://www.merckmanuals.com/home/immune-disorders/immunodeficiency-disorders/hyper-igm-syndrome

Symptoms and impact on daily life

The hardest part of this disease is that it doesn't cause symptoms at first — it progresses silently, and by the time symptoms show up, it's already quite advanced. The symptoms are shortness of breath with exertion, a dry and persistent cough, wheezing when breathing, exhaustion, lower physical endurance, and very frequent respiratory infectionsAmerican Lung Association. (2025). Bronchiolitis obliterans symptoms and diagnosis. American Lung Association. https://www.lung.org/lung-health-diseases/lung-disease-lookup/popcorn-lung/symptoms-diagnosisCleveland Clinic. (2026). Popcorn lung (bronchiolitis obliterans). Cleveland Clinic. https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22590-popcorn-lung-bronchiolitis-obliterans

In everyday life, you feel it when climbing stairs, running, exercising, or doing other activities.

How it is diagnosed

There are several ways; I was diagnosed through a biopsy, which, as a fun fact, wasn't just a simple biopsy — they actually broke my left lung. Diagnosing it is also usually based on lung function tests, like spirometry, and doctors may also order chest CT scans, X-rays, and bronchoscopies. Catching it early matters a lot, because it allows treatment to start before the damage progresses too far.American Lung Association. (2025). Bronchiolitis obliterans symptoms and diagnosis. Treatment

There is no treatment that can reverse the damage. However, there are treatments to try to slow the progression, reduce inflammation, and improve quality of life.Cleveland Clinic. (2026). Popcorn lung (bronchiolitis obliterans). Cleveland Clinic. https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22590-popcorn-lung-bronchiolitis-obliterans

One of the most commonly used regimens is FAM therapy, made up of inhaled fluticasone, azithromycin, and montelukast. Depending on each patient's progress, the medical team may consider other treatments, like immunosuppressants — for example, I took prednisolone, and it's been one of the worst things I've taken, because it made me swell up completely, and I still have after-effects from it today. Other options include exercise, but at your own pace.Dinh-Van L. (2026). Cure

It is a disease with no cure, because the damage it leaves in the bronchioles is permanent. Even so, early diagnosis, frequent check-ups, proper treatment, and preventing infections can help preserve lung function for longer.Cleveland Clinic. (2026). Popcorn lung (bronchiolitis obliterans). Cleveland Clinic. https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/22590-popcorn-lung-bronchiolitis-obliterans

Living with this disease means learning to recognize when your body can't take any more, knowing your limits, but without giving up a normal life.

Objective

To understand, from a different angle, bronchiolitis obliterans as a complication of chronic graft-versus-host rejection following a bone marrow transplant — from its origin, to its medical management, to the impact it has on everyday life.

  • To teach visitors to the site what bronchiolitis obliterans is and how it relates to transplants, in this case bone marrow transplants, so they understand a disease that is little known but on the rise because of vape use
  • To establish what relationship exists between an average person's lifestyle habits and respiratory risk factors, in order to reflect on which everyday practices can prevent or worsen this type of disease
  • To generate empathy and awareness about what it is like to live day to day with this disease, showing the role that medical and family support plays in managing it.
